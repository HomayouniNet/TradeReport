Macro backdrop

Rates and yields remain a headwind for speculative assets. FRED’s effective federal funds rate was 3.88% in October, up from 3.63% in July. The 10-year Treasury yield stood at 4.91% on November 24, versus 4.61% on July 28, though below its late-September high of 5.29%. Higher yields can raise the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets such as BTC-USD and tighten financial conditions; a sustained decline in yields could ease that pressure.

Inflation indexes continued to rise. CPI increased from 333.979 in May to 337.476 in October; core PCE rose from 129.796 to 131.312 over the same period. These are index-level changes, not annual inflation rates. The sequence nevertheless shows continued price increases in the reported data, which may constrain expectations for easier policy.

The labor reading is comparatively resilient. Unemployment was 4.1% in October, down from 4.3% in May. That does not by itself establish the likely policy path, but it offers no clear labor-market signal of urgent easing.

The yield curve remained modestly positive. The 10-year minus 2-year spread was 0.24 percentage point on November 25, down from 0.35 in late July. It was not inverted in the latest observation, but the decline is worth monitoring alongside the increase in longer yields.

December Fed expectations: treat with care

Polymarket showed a 94% probability of a 25-basis-point Fed rate increase after the December meeting, resolving December 9; the 50-basis-point-hike outcome was at 2%, while a cut was near zero. These are live prediction-market prices, not official guidance or a settled forecast. Given the size of the implied hike and the FRED rate series available only through October, verify the pricing and upcoming policy context independently before using it as a trading premise. If markets continue to price tighter policy and yields rise, that would generally be a negative backdrop for BTC-USD; a repricing toward easier policy could support relief.
