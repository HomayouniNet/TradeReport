A first improvement would be a reclaim and hold above the **10-day EMA near $62,189**, preferably accompanied by improving MACD momentum and stronger closes. That alone would not reverse the broader trend: the **$72,027–$75,130** 200- and 50-day SMA zone remains a much higher hurdle. Treat those averages as reference areas, not validated resistance or support.

For risk management, the roughly **$3,007 ATR** argues for sizing positions conservatively and allowing for volatility. Any short-term long taken on oversold conditions is countertrend until price and momentum improve; waiting for confirmation reduces reliance on RSI alone, though it may mean entering later.
