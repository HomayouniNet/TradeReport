## BTC-USD — technical read for 2026-11-24

**Overall picture:** BTC-USD is in a pronounced short- and medium-term downtrend, with momentum indicators still bearish. RSI is deeply oversold, and BTC-USD remains above the lower Bollinger Band, so a relief rally is possible—but neither condition alone confirms a reversal. Elevated ATR also argues for smaller position sizes and wider, volatility-aware risk limits.

### Actionable, conditional approach

**For trend-following traders:** The prevailing evidence favors caution on bullish entries while BTC-USD remains below the 10-day EMA and MACD remains below its signal line. A close back above the 10-day EMA would be an initial short-term improvement, but would not by itself reverse the broader picture while price remains below the 50- and 200-day SMAs.

**For reversal-oriented traders:** RSI below 30 makes a relief move plausible, but confirmation matters. Look for price stabilization and improving momentum—such as MACD narrowing toward its signal line—rather than buying solely because RSI is oversold. The verified lower Bollinger Band at $55,145.53 is a band reference, not guaranteed support.

**For risk management:** Use ATR to calibrate exposure and stop distance to the strategy and entry; at roughly $2,992, even a one-ATR move is meaningful. Avoid treating that figure as a recommended stop price without an entry, timeframe, and risk budget.
