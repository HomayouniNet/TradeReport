A higher risk-free yield can compete with non-yielding speculative assets and may tighten financial conditions, all else equal.

The reported **10-year minus 2-year Treasury spread was +0.24 percentage points on November 25**, narrower than +0.39 in late August. It remained positive in the latest reading, but the narrowing is worth monitoring as a macro signal; it is not, by itself, a timing indicator for XRP-USD.

The latest available FRED CPI index was **337.476 for October**, up from 336.298 in September. The core PCE price index was **131.312 in October**, up from 130.929 in September.
