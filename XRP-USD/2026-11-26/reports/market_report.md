The lower Bollinger Band at **$0.847343** is a useful volatility reference, not a guaranteed support level. Recent observed lows include **$0.894632 on 14 November** and **$0.884004 on 20 November**; those are historical prints, not proof that the area will hold if revisited.

**Early stabilization:** A move back above the 10 EMA, followed by a sustained reclaim of the Bollinger middle band at **$0.989635**, would improve the short-term picture. That would still leave XRP-USD below the **50 SMA at $1.18024**, so it would be evidence of recovery rather than confirmation that the medium-term trend had turned.

**Stronger trend-reversal evidence:** Traders looking for a more substantial change could require price to reclaim and hold above the 50 SMA, alongside improving momentum. The **200 SMA at $1.22911** is a further long-term benchmark. Because these moving averages lag price, their levels are context rather than precise entry signals.

For risk management, use ATR to calibrate position size and stop distance to current volatility; an ATR-based distance is a planning reference, not a guarantee against gaps, rapid moves, or execution slippage. Avoid treating the recent oversold readings or improving histogram as a standalone buy signal. XRP-USD is a crypto asset, and this technical snapshot does not assess company fundamentals.
