- **For a cautious bullish setup:** Look first for XRP-USD to reclaim and hold above the 10-day EMA near **0.94987**, then the Bollinger middle band at **0.97883**. A stronger confirmation would be sustained closes above the middle band alongside continued positive MACD histogram readings and an RSI recovery. Even then, the 50-day SMA at **1.17157** and 200-day SMA at **1.22646** remain substantial trend-repair hurdles.
- **For a bearish continuation setup:** Failure to recover the short-term averages, followed by renewed weakness, would leave the downtrend intact. The recent close at **0.909707 on 2026-11-20** is a useful recent reference, not proof of support. The Bollinger lower band at **0.85960** is another volatility-based reference, not a guaranteed floor.
- **Avoid over-interpreting the current bounce attempt.** The positive MACD histogram is an improvement, but the close remains below the major averages and RSI remains weak. A reversal thesis would be stronger with price confirmation, not just an oversold-style momentum reading or a single indicator crossover.
