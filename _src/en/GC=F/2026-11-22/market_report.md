**Base case: cautious, with a downward lean.** While price is under the 10-day EMA and the 50- and 200-day averages, the trend evidence still favors sellers. If trading trend continuation, do not chase the drop immediately after one high-volume session. A break of the 20 November low (**3,502.90**), or a failed rebound that turns back under the lower band, can be a condition to examine. Neither guarantees further decline.

**Rebound case: only with confirmation.** Oversold RSI and a close under the lower band raise the chance of a corrective bounce. After that, the 10-day EMA (**3,805.41**) is a more important filter for a change in momentum. A brief pierce of these levels is not confirmation. These data alone are not a sufficient basis for one definitive turning point or one stop that fits every trader.
